# 🎵 MS Music Studio - Standalone macOS & Windows Application

Welcome to **MS Music Studio**! This is a native application (just like VS Code, Slack, or Spotify) built specifically for your dad to easily edit and maintain the official **Muralidhar Shenoy Music Website** on **macOS** and **Windows** without writing any code.

---

## 🖥️ How to Access & Launch the Mac App

### Option A: From Applications Folder (Recommended)
1. Drag the **`MS Music Studio.app`** file from the project folder into your Mac's **`/Applications`** folder.
2. Open **Finder** -> **Applications** (or use **Launchpad** / **Spotlight Search** `Cmd + Space` -> type `MS Music Studio`).
3. Click to open **MS Music Studio** just like VS Code!

### Option B: From the Project Directory
1. Open the project folder in **Finder**.
2. Double-click **`MS Music Studio.app`**.

---

## ✨ Features & What You Can Edit

### 1. 👁️ Visual Studio & Live Site Preview
- Switch seamlessly between **Visual Studio Editor** (to edit content) and **Full Site Preview** (to view real-time site design).

### 2. 🌟 Content Editors
- **Hero Banner**: Edit Name, Profession Title, Taglines, and upload Hero Background pictures & Signature logos.
- **About Artist**: Edit biography paragraphs, hometown, corporate background, and portrait photos.
- **Music Tracks**: Add audio tracks, upload MP3/WAV files, set genres, languages, and Spotify links.
- **Concerts & Events**: Add upcoming concert dates, venues, timing, and ticket availability.
- **Photo Gallery**: Upload performance photos, studio captures, and captions.
- **Posts & Social Links**: Manage YouTube videos, Instagram reels, Facebook posts, and email/booking forms.

### 3. 📜 Version History & 1-Click Revert
- Go to **`📜 Version History & Revert`** in the studio sidebar.
- Browse all past published updates and click **`Revert to this Version`** to restore any older version instantly.

### 4. 🔀 Merge Conflict Resolution Wizard
- If a conflict occurs during version restoration or syncing, the app automatically opens the **Conflict Resolution Wizard**.
- Choose between:
  - 🅰️ **Keep Current Local Version**
  - 🅱️ **Use Restored / Selected Version**
  - ❌ **Cancel & Abort Revert**

---

## 🌐 How to Publish Your Changes Live

1. Click the glowing golden **`Publish to Website`** button at top right.
2. Click **`🚀 Push Live Now`**.
3. The app automatically saves files, commits to **Git**, pushes to **GitHub**, and deploys live to **GitHub Pages**!
